Research ethics decision game — private, in your browser

How it works. You play the researcher. At each decision point, pick a course of action and the committee responds. Nothing you do here is saved anywhere but this browser — and you can wipe it in one click.

Run the study. Face the committee.

Pick a scenario and you become the researcher — every design choice has an ethical cost or a payoff. Each decision point ends with feedback grounded in the BPS Code of Human Research Ethics, and the scenario closes with the committee's verdict on your protocol.

Learn the principles

The six principles the simulator scores you on, plus two special cases students ask about most: when deception is ever justified and working with vulnerable groups. Grounded in the BPS Code of Human Research Ethics (2022) and UK GDPR practice.

My report

Your decision record across every scenario you've attempted — your choices beside the strongest ones, a principle-by-principle tally, and reflection prompts for seminars or supervision. Export it as HTML, or wipe it.

Ethics Simulator — built by Joshua Bellot (MSc Psychology (Conversion), Manchester Metropolitan University) for the Psych & Me toolkit. Teaching aid only: scenarios are fictional composites and feedback paraphrases good practice, not legal advice. Always consult your institution's ethics committee and current BPS guidance.
